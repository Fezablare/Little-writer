# Drill style

Rules for new writing and maths drills in Little Writer. Read this before adding or rewriting weeks.

The reader is a Year 1–2 child. Australian spelling: favourite, Mum, colour. Sentences should be short enough to read aloud. One topic teaches one skill. Do not sneak in a second new rule on the boss item.

Topics are not calendar weeks. A child stays until the topic feels easy. The live list is `js/programme.js`.

## Weekly shape

Every writing week uses the same order. The tasks must teach the same rule as the lesson.

1. **Rule.** Child language, one sentence. Example: who comes before what they did.
2. **Model.** One good sentence and one broken twin, with why. The broken twin is the week's error family, not a random slip.
3. **Warm-up.** Copies the model. The model can stay in view.
4. **Practice.** Same rule, new people or place.
5. **Boss.** Same rule, new scene. Do not add a second new rule.
6. **Write.** The child composes. Show a success check they can tick, in the same words as the rule.

Layers in code stay `L(warm, practice, boss)`: two warm items, two practice items, one boss item.

## Three error families

Name the family in the prompt. Do not mix families in one item unless the week is an explicit review (week 18).

| Family | The sentence already has… | What is wrong | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unfinished | nothing complete | no who, no action, or a hanging When / After / Because | Running to the park. |
| Wrong job | who and an action | order is flipped, or a paint word sits after its noun | The cake ate Mia. |
| Finish marks | a clear thought | capital, full stop, or question mark only | mum packed my lunch |

Early weeks may pair a finish mark with one other family, but the prompt must say which one you are hunting. Weeks 16–17 teach unfinished thoughts. Week 18 may mix families. Do not spend a structure week only on capitals, stops, or past tense.

## What each task may do

Use kinds the app already renders: story, choose, build, spot, proof, expand, arrange, write. Do not invent a kind unless the app is updated to show it.

| Kind | Job | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| story | Show | A real `story.text` to read first. Cloze `{{gaps}}` come after. | Theme title with no story. Gaps that are the only "story". |
| choose | Share | 3 options. One clearly right. Distractors are the week's error family. | Silly options that no child would pick. A new rule hiding in the boss choice. |
| build | Share | Shuffled tiles. One exact answer. Who before the action. | Tiles already in the right order. |
| expand | Try | A pale or unfinished seed. Hint names the job to add: who, paint word, or opener. | Failing a good extra detail that still has who and did. |
| write | Try | 4–5 prompts. Starters may be blank. Several good sentences are fine. | Always handing them the subject. One secret wording. |
| spot / proof | Look again | After the child has met the rule in story, choose, or build. `guide` before the fix, `explain` after. Fragments accept several rewrites (`answers`, `mustInclude`). | First meeting of a new idea. One rigid sentence when many fixes are right. |

Success check for write and expand, in child words:

- Did I name who?
- Did I say what they did?
- Did I finish the thought (stop or question mark)?

Add a fourth check only if it is that week's skill (paint word, opener, join, one topic).

## Who

Who can be a person, a job, a group, an animal, or a thing: Mum, the nurse, Everyone, a bee, the lamp. Put who before the action.

Do not reuse the same cast across weeks. Sam, Mia, and "tiny frog" may appear once, then change the scene. A later week should not repair the same broken line as an earlier week.

## Maths

Times-tables missions and addition stay separate. Addition is typed totals, 2-digit and 3-digit, about 20 questions a pack. No timers.

## Later

A later pass may tie every drill to one tiny text type per week (list, joined ideas, paragraph, then edit that same paragraph). Do not start that rewrite unless it is the task. New drills should still avoid random sentences that do not belong to the week's story.
